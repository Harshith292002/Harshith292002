## About Me

I'm an AI/ML engineer at the intersection of **deep learning**, **biomedical signal processing**, and **medical imaging**. Currently pursuing my MSE in ECE at Johns Hopkins (GPA 3.8/4.0) with a concentration in AI, ML, and Generative Deep Learning.

- 🔬 **Research**: Built **MAISI-US** — the first adaptation of NVIDIA's MAISI 3D latent diffusion model to ultrasound-to-CT cross-modality synthesis for intraoperative spine surgery navigation (ISTAR Lab, JHU)
- 🧠 **Industry**: Designing wearable sleep-staging algorithms (transformer + knowledge distillation) and IMU-based pose estimation at **Neurava**
- 📡 **Signal Processing**: ECG-based respiratory rate estimation (2.5 bpm MAE), CEEMDAN/wavelet pipelines for clinical wearables
- 🛠️ **Builder**: GPU-accelerated batch inference pipelines, 3D Slicer modules, Qt tooling, and MLOps infrastructure (W&B, Docker, AWS)
